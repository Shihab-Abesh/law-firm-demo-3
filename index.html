    Questions

    Common questions

    Straight answers about working with our chamber.

    Do you offer a free first consultation?

    Yes. Your first consultation is free and confidential. We listen to your situation, review any documents you have and explain your options before you decide anything.

    What should I bring to my first meeting?

    Bring any documents connected to your matter — deeds, agreements, notices, FIR or case papers, correspondence — and a photo ID. If you are unsure, bring everything; we will sort through it with you.

    How are your fees structured?

    After the first consultation we give you a clear, written estimate. Depending on the matter, fees may be fixed, staged or based on court appearances. There are no hidden charges.

    Which courts do you appear in?

    We represent clients before the District and Sessions Courts, special tribunals, and the High Court Division and Appellate Division of the Supreme Court of Bangladesh.

    Can you help if I live abroad?

    Yes. We regularly act for non-resident Bangladeshis. Meetings can take place by phone or video, documents can be signed through a power of attorney, and updates are shared online.

    How will I know what is happening in my case?

    You receive updates after every hearing and whenever there is a development. You can also contact your barrister directly by phone, email or WhatsApp.
